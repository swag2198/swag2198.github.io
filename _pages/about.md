Hi! I am Swagatam, it literally translates to “welcome” in several languages!

I am working on the <span class="text-highlight">[OpenEuroLLM](https://openeurollm.eu/)</span> project at the [ELLIS Institute Tübingen](https://institute-tue.ellis.eu/). I am part of the team led by [Dr. Aaron Klein](https://aaronkl.github.io/) and [Dr. David Salinas](https://geoalgo.github.io/). See [research](#research) below for my current interests.

I completed a master's degree in Machine Learning at the [University of Tübingen](https://uni-tuebingen.de/fakultaeten/mathematisch-naturwissenschaftliche-fakultaet/fachbereiche/informatik/studium/studierende/lehre-studienorganisation/studiengaenge/machine-learning/). Prior to that, I worked as a research engineer at [IBM Research India](https://research.ibm.com/labs/india) in Bangalore. I received my B. Tech degrees in Electrical Engineering (major) and Computer Science (minor) from the [Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Kharagpur](https://www.iitkgp.ac.in/) in 2020.

If you are interested in my work or would like to chat about common interests, potential collaboration etc., I would genuinely love to hear from you, feel free to shoot me an email.

For more, see [research](#research), [latest news](#news), and [socials](#social) below.
